Contact exchange privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Deus Intus receives the contact details you choose to provide when exchanging business cards. This includes your name, phone number and/or email, and any optional company or conversation notes.

Why we collect it

We use your information to follow up on our business conversation and manage professional contacts. We collect it only after you choose to share it using the consent checkbox.

Where it is stored

Details are sent securely to our own n8n workflow and stored in a private contact table, not in the public GitHub Pages website. We do not publish or sell them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by emailing deus.intus.111@gmail.com. We keep contact details only as needed for appropriate business follow-up and review the records periodically.

Technical details

The public business card is a static web app. Submitted form data is sent over HTTPS. The app does not require you to create an account, and it does not run advertising trackers.
